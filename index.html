Kids Guardian Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026

Kids Guardian is a parental-control app for Android. A parent installs it on a device used by their child to set allowed apps, daily screen time, bedtime, and breaks. This policy explains what the app handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • Kids Guardian has no account, no server, and no cloud service.
  • The app does not request internet access. It cannot send data anywhere.
  • Everything it stores stays on the device it is installed on.
  • We do not collect, sell, or share any personal or usage data.
  • There are no ads and no analytics or tracking libraries.

What is stored on the device

  • The child's first name or nickname, as entered by the parent.
  • The parent PIN, stored only as a salted hash (never as text), encrypted with an Android Keystore key when available.
  • The parent's settings: allowed apps, limits, schedules, and device restrictions.
  • Daily screen-time totals and per-app usage, kept for 90 days and then deleted.
  • A history of parent overrides, such as extra time or unlocks.

Uninstalling Kids Guardian or resetting the device deletes all of this data.

Accessibility service

When the app is not the device owner, the parent may turn on Kids Guardian's accessibility service ("Standard protection"). Before it is turned on, the app shows what the service does and asks for the parent's consent.

  • What it reads: which app is on screen, and the text of Android Settings screens only to find the name Kids Guardian.
  • Why: to cover apps the parent has not allowed, to apply the daily limit, bedtime, and breaks, and to stop Kids Guardian from being turned off or uninstalled without the parent PIN.
  • What it does not do: it does not record, store, or share screen content, messages, or typing. Nothing leaves the device.

Other permissions

  • Usage access: measures how long apps are used, to apply screen-time limits.
  • List of installed apps: lets the parent choose which apps the child may use.
  • Device administrator or device owner: applies the parent's restrictions and prevents removal of the app without the PIN.
  • Display over other apps (optional): shows the parent-unlock countdown.
  • Notifications and starting at boot: keep protection running and show its status.

Children

Kids Guardian is used by parents and guardians. It does not collect personal information from children. The child's name and usage history are entered or measured on the device, stay on the device, and are visible only to the parent through the PIN-protected parent area.

Changes
